Privacy Policy

Personal eBay Inventory Manager

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Information We Access

This application accesses eBay account and listing information only after the account owner authorizes access through eBay. This may include listing titles, descriptions, prices, quantities, item identifiers, and listing status.

How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to help the account owner review, organize, and manage their eBay inventory.

Sharing and Security

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Information may be processed through eBay APIs and GitHub Pages hosting infrastructure to operate the application. Reasonable measures are used to protect information.

Data Retention

Information is retained only as needed to provide the application's inventory-management functions. The account owner may revoke the application's eBay access through their eBay account settings.
